02 Teaching approach

No two students learn the same way. No two lessons should be the same.

  1. i.

    Customized to the student

    Age, experience and personality all shape how a child learns. I get to know each student, then build lessons around them.

  2. ii.

    Fun comes first

    Children learn best when they are enjoying themselves. I keep lessons engaging, so piano is something they look forward to.

  3. iii.

    Passion that lasts

    A child who enjoys the piano keeps coming back to it. That is what keeps students playing for years.

03 Lessons at home

The studio comes to you.

You do not travel to me. I travel to you, and the lesson takes place at your own piano.

  • No travel time

    No drive across town and no sitting in traffic. The lesson simply becomes part of your day at home.

  • A familiar setting

    Students learn in a place where they already feel comfortable, which matters especially for children.

  • Their own instrument

    Lessons happen on the same instrument the student practices on between visits.

07 Questions

What parents usually ask.

Where do lessons take place?

In your home. I travel to you, so there is no travel time for your family.

What ages do you teach?

Students of any age are welcome. Most of my students are children.

Do you teach complete beginners?

Yes. Lessons are beginner friendly, and many students are brand new to piano when they start.

How long and how often are lessons?

Two lessons a week are recommended, though it depends on the student. For younger students, sessions are usually 30 or 45 minutes to help them stay focused. I am flexible with timing and happy to arrange what suits your family.

Which areas do you serve?

Families across the Seattle area. Send an inquiry with your location and I will confirm.

When are lessons available?

My schedule is flexible. Share the days and times that suit your family in the inquiry form and we will find a time that works.

How do I find out about lesson details and rates?

Send an inquiry. I respond within 24 hours with everything you need to know, based on the student’s age and experience.
